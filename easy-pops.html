branding//creative

EASY POPS is a playful and vibrant sparkling fruit water brand designed to bring a splash of fun to everyday refreshment.

The visual identity combines bold typography, energetic color palettes, abstract patterns, and quirky hand-drawn fruit illustrations to create a fresh, youthful, and expressive look. Each flavor has its own unique personality while maintaining a cohesive brand identity. The result is a bold and eye catching packaging experience that celebrates creativity, individuality, and the joy of keeping things simple.
