Urban Oasis is a boutique hotel located in the heart of Tel Aviv, offering a personalized hospitality experience in a vibrant urban environment.

The project focuses on designing a comprehensive management dashboard for the hotel's shift manager, bringing together daily operations, guest requests, room statuses, and staff coordination into one intuitive interface.

The Challenge

Managing a hotel involves juggling multiple departments, constantly changing information, and unexpected situations that require immediate attention.

The challenge was to organize a large amount of operational data into a clear, intuitive interface, allowing shift managers to quickly understand what's happening, identify priorities, and take action without feeling overwhelmed.

The Solution

The design focused on transforming complex operational data into a clear, centralized dashboard through a carefully structured information hierarchy.

The interface prioritizes critical, time-sensitive information, such as urgent notifications, room occupancy, and daily check-ins and check-outs, followed by staff availability and departmental operations. Secondary information is organized into dedicated sections, keeping the dashboard focused without sacrificing functionality. A modular layout, clear typography, and purposeful use of color create distinct levels of visual importance, enabling quick scanning, easy navigation, and efficient decision-making.
