Peres Center for Peace and Innovation is a leading Israeli nonprofit organization dedicated to inspiring creativity, innovation, and positive social change. Through its educational outreach programs, the center brings interactive workshops directly into schools, encouraging students to think outside the box, explore new ideas, and develop creative problem solving skills. By combining hands-on learning, collaboration, and innovative thinking, these workshops empower the next generation to dream big and turn ideas into meaningful change.

The off-site workshops are conducted for 7th and 8th-grade students at their own schools. The primary challenge was finding a way to make this knowledge accessible to a young audience outside the center’s walls.
A solution was needed that could captivate and excite a generation that loses focus quickly and craves constant stimulation.

Hey! I'm Percy and I'm going to join this ride

Downloading the app prior to the workshop builds engagement and interest even before the team arrives in the classroom. Furthermore, through an introductory quiz, students discover what type of entrepreneur they resemble and who will be in their group.
